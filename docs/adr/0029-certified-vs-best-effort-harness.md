# Certified vs Best-Effort Harnesses

Cognibrain will distinguish Certified Harnesses from Best-Effort Harnesses. A Certified Harness requires trace-backed, mutation-tested Harness Conformance; unsupported or untraceable adapters may still operate as Best-Effort Harnesses, but they cannot claim the full Brain Lifecycle or Mistake Prevention Claim. This protects the market promise without blocking experimental or partially capable integrations.
