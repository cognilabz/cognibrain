# Mistake Replay Proof for V1 Claim

Cognibrain V1 will prove its Mistake Prevention Claim through Mistake Replay Proof: real corrected Product Mistakes are replayed through a Certified Harness, and the product must show the agent avoids repeating them. This rejects a benchmark leaderboard, broad brain-behavior demo, or single-harness-only showcase as the primary V1 proof because those can look impressive while failing to prove the market promise directly.
