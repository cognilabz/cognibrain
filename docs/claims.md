# Claim-To-Test Evidence Map

Public claims in README, PRODUCT, docs, and release notes should map to one of these evidence gates. If a claim cannot point at a gate, keep it as roadmap language instead of production language.

## Explicit Non-Claims

The 2026-05-25 strict Plan1_3 re-audit keeps these out of production language until real evidence exists:

- Managed SaaS readiness, uptime, autoscaling, billing, SSO, hosted support, or vendor certification.
- Real customer-repository coding-agent performance from CogniCodeBench alone; CogniCodeBench is synthetic simulator proof.
- Competitor leadership from internal synthetic ablations alone; comparable artifacts must use the same dataset, metrics, top-K or budget, and runner contract. Do not claim vendor leadership without that evidence.
- Tenant-specific GitHub, Slack, Discord, Jira, Confluence, Linear, Notion, Google, or other connector certification without fresh deployment credentials and smoke artifacts.
- Vendor-hosted competitor leadership without same-dataset, same-metric, same-budget artifacts and explicit proof level.
- Indexed pgvector production search or unmanaged target-cluster certification without deployment pooler, encryption key management, backup verification, and target-environment `verify:postgres` evidence.
